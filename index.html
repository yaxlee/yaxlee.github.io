Research

I'm interested in localization, SLAM, sensor fusion, and 3D perception: helping robots know where they are and what is around them, especially when GNSS is degraded and the scene is changing. Most of my work fuses cameras, IMUs, LiDAR, and GNSS. Some papers are highlighted.

DGVI-SLAM: DEM-Aided Visual-Inertial SLAM with Bounded GNSS Recovery
Yaxuan Li, [co-authors]
ICRA, 2027 (under review)
paper (coming soon)

Fusing camera, IMU, GNSS, and a digital elevation model yields globally referenced localization that survives degraded GNSS in cities with varying elevation, cutting median position RMSE by 30% across six urban sequences.

CVD-SfM: Cross-View Deep Front-end SfM for Multi-Altitude Pose Estimation
Yaxuan Li, [co-authors]
IROS, 2025
project page / arXiv / code

A cross-view transformer and a deep front-end (DISK + LightGlue) let structure-from-motion register ground and aerial images to satellite imagery, producing geo-referenced reconstructions and poses across altitudes.

SGM-SLAM: Scene Graph Matching for Data-Efficient Distributed SLAM
Yaxuan Li, [co-authors]
[Venue], [Year]
paper / code

Matching multi-layer scene graphs built from LiDAR and OneFormer semantics enables data-efficient multi-robot SLAM, reaching 1.32 m (outdoor) and 0.81 m (indoor) ATE versus 3.92 m and 3.36 m for Swarm-SLAM on real Livox Mid-360 data.

Dynamic-Aware SuperPoint: A Robust Feature Extractor for Visual SLAM
[authors, including Yaxuan Li]
[Venue], [Year]
paper

Augmenting SuperPoint to classify each interest point as static, quasi-static, or dynamic makes visual SLAM more robust in scenes with moving objects.

Visual-Assisted Localization in Dynamic Plant Environments (Localization Team, sole developer)
Built a visual global localization module on top of LiDAR-inertial SLAM (Cartographer) for initialization and re-localization. Extended NVIDIA cuVGL on Jetson AGX Orin with SuperPoint/LightGlue and 3D-map PnP, and trained custom PyTorch models for commercial deployment. Reached 3 cm accuracy at 0.3 s/frame on the deployed robot.

3D LiDAR Localization (Localization Team)
Deployed and benchmarked FAST-LIO on an Ouster 128-channel LiDAR for next-gen AMRs, building keyframe-based local submaps and registering them with GICP.

3D LiDAR Object Detection and Segmentation (Perception Team, sole developer)
Exported mmdetection3d models to TensorRT and benchmarked them on Jetson AGX Orin. Built an annotated real-world plant dataset in CVAT and synthetic plant data with dynamic pedestrians and obstacles in Isaac Sim.
